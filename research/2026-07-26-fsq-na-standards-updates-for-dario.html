🟠 Changing soon (new edition / rule within ~12 months)

  • ISO 9001 → ISO 9001:2026 (DIS out; final ~Sep 2026). This pulls EN 9100 → IA9100A (~early 2027) and IATF 16949 (new ed. ~2027) along with it.
  • IEC 61508 → Edition 3 (~early 2027) — the parent functional-safety standard.
  • IEC 62304 → Edition 2 (~2026): widens to all health software, replaces A/B/C classes with two rigour levels, adds an AI/ML lifecycle.
  • ISO 12100 → Edition 2 (at Draft International Standard — "within coming months").
  • IEC 63187-1 (defence system safety; on site as IEC TR 63187) — first edition scheduled ~Sep 2026.
  • IEC 62443 series — active -4-1/-4-2 amendments + new parts, aligning to the EU Cyber Resilience Act.
  • TISAX / VDA-ISA → ISA2027 catalogue (effective for assessments ordered from 1 Jan 2027).
  • EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 — applies 20 Jan 2027 (harmonised-standards list expected end-2026).
  • EASA CS-23 / CS-25 / SC-VTOL / SORA — continuous amendments (CS-23 Amdt 6, May 2026; SORA 2.5, Sept 2025).
  • EASA AI Concept Paper → Issue 3 (consultation closes 12 Aug 2026; moving toward binding AMC).
  • IEC 63452 (rail cybersecurity) — first edition expected ~2027.
  • ISO 10218-1/-2:2025 — already published (Ed 3), absorbing ISO/TS 15066's cobot content.

🟡 Watch (revision active but further out, or pending regulation)

  • ISO 26262 → Edition 3 (early Working Draft); ISO/SAE 21434 (under systematic review); ISO 21448 SOTIF (flagged "to be revised").
  • UNECE R155 / R156 (active amendments; R156 new 01-series); ISO 25119 → Ed 3.
  • DO-254 → DO-254A (pub ~Dec 2027); DO-178C gains a new DO-395 COTS/open-source supplement.
  • ISO 13849 (Part 2 revision + planned merge); IEC 62061 (Amendment 2, 2026); MIL-STD-882E → MIL-STD-882F (draft, no date).
  • EN 50126 (A1:2024 + draft -4/-5 parts); EN 50716 (prA1:2026 amendment).
  • ISO/PAS 8800 (PAS → future full IS); ANSI/UL 4600 (revision in committee); ISO/IEC TR 5469 → normative successor ISO/IEC TS 22440.
  • EU MDR 2017/745 (Commission revision proposal, 16 Dec 2025); MDCG 2019-16 (affected by that revision); IEC 60601-1 → Edition 4 (early).
  • NIS2 (national transposition still completing); ISO/IEC 23894 & 42001 (text stable; EU AI Act harmonised-standards alignment ~Q4 2026).
  • Automotive SPICE — v4.0 current, v3.1 sunsetting (assessors must upgrade); new A-SPICE for Cybersecurity v2.0 (2025).

🟢 Stable (re-confirmed, no active change)

  • ISO 13485, ISO 14971, IEC 62366-1 (all re-confirmed on systematic review), ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (+Amd 1:2024), ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023.
  • DO-331, DO-278A, Def Stan 00-56, MDCG's underlying standards, CE / LVD 2014/35 / EMC 2014/30 (directives themselves), SN 29500.
  • MDCG 2019-16, ISO 60601-4-5, ISO/IEC 42001 texts unchanged (watched only for external regulatory anchoring).
